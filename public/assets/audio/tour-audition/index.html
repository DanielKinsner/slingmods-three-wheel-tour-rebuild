SlingMods / Three-Wheel Tour / Audio candidate

A world you can hear.

Cinematic electronica, tangible workshop details, two distinct vehicles and room for the road. This listening room is independent of the game's other worktrees.

Start with the full mix.

The walkthrough below records the actual game audio service after its master limiter. It uses a scripted telemetry fixture to move from showroom to wet Harbor driving, then the Ryker and a result. It is an audio demonstration, not a completed race.

Individual files below play at their authored asset level; the game balances them more quietly and dynamically. Only one preview plays at a time. In the game, Sound mix → Music off disables the score while retaining vehicle and world sound.
