# Six scoped Spyder upgrades

All geometry is a Blender-authored reference approximation mounted to the purchased custom vehicle, not manufacturer CAD or physical fit certification. Visual year is unknown; the selected catalog baseline is a US **2023 base F3**. Credits are game currency, not retail prices. No products were purchased during implementation.

| Upgrade | Exact scoped option and source | Game behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Pedal Commander | SM-7386, 2014–2023 connector; [product](https://www.slingmods.com/pedal-commander/canam-spyder-throttle-response-controller-pedal-commander) | Four response curves; no peak-power gain. Reachable controller below the custom bars. |
| Elka front | SM-20956 / 70003, F3/F3-S Stage 4 pair, 36 mm bodies; [product](https://www.slingmods.com/canam-spyder-f3-f3s-front-shocks-coilovers-elka-suspension) | Front manual preload, rebound and low-speed compression; rear independent. |
| Elka rear | SM-20957 / 70007, F3/F3-S Stage 4, 46 mm body, manual preload; [product](https://www.slingmods.com/canam-spyder-f3-f3s-rear-shock-coilover-elka-suspension) | Separate rear manual preload/rebound/low-speed compression. No hydraulic preload or high-speed knob. |
| Baja Ron ULTRA | SM-18320 plus the required SM-18298 aluminum end-link pair; [bar](https://www.slingmods.com/canam-spyder-f3-st-rs-ultra-performance-sway-bar-kit-baja-ron), [links](https://www.slingmods.com/baja-ron/canam-spyder-sway-bar-end-links-baja-ron) | One bundle/charge; conservative front support redistribution, no hidden tire grip. |
| TricLED F3 UnderGlow | SM-18631; [product](https://www.slingmods.com/canam-spyder-f3-chaser-underglow-lighting-kit-tricled), [installation manual](https://www.slingmods.com/pdf/canam-spyder-f3-chaser-underglow-led-lighting-kit-installation-instructions.pdf) | Two inner-grille, two upper-headlamp, two side-frame and two lower-A-arm strips adapted to actual custom surfaces. The 2024+ headlamp exception is excluded. Shared color/brightness/on-off and a supported steady/pulse subset; no physics effects. |
| TricLED wheel lights | SM-16776, front pair, standalone controller; [product](https://www.slingmods.com/canam-spyder-chaser-wheel-light-kit) | Four arcs per stationary ring, attached to Brembo/steering carriers, not spinning wheels. No combined-controller purchase assumption or physics effects. |

Wheel-light clearance: 180 samples of the selected ring path against the actual rotating front mesh found about 30.1 mm minimum clearance after allowing a 7 mm support-tube radius. The ring and caliper remain in the same steering/travel frame. This is a game-geometry check; it is not tire deflection, thermal, manufacturing tolerance or road-use approval. Custom frame strip routing differs from stock hardware, and some tube paths are visually approximate.

All six fit in both free preview and the earned career workshop. Install/remove restores stock visibility; paint, controllers and normalized adjustments save with the Spyder recipe. Ownership, credits, immutable career entries, Time Attack ghosts and challenge records preserve vehicle/build/tune identity. Existing Slingshot and Ryker records are not relabeled.

Shared Tour medal thresholds are retained and clearly labeled as Tour targets. This work validates identity separation and genuine result recording; it does not claim that every top medal has been recalibrated or earned with every Spyder build.
