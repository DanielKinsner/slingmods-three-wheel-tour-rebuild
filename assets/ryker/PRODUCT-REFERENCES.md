References checked September 22, 2026. The mounted meshes are original game approximations based on these references, not manufacturer CAD, fitment certification, or a product-performance test. Downloaded reference photographs are kept only in the local research cache, not shipped as game artwork. No purchase or external cart was created.

Panther plus TricLED is **not verified retail compatibility**. The game deliberately omits the stock-grille strips when Panther is fitted, and retains the independent headlight and arm strips. Removing Panther restores those original stock-grille strips. This limitation is also in the player-facing product details.

Parts are reversible in free preview and career. Free preview never grants ownership. Career uses its own earned-credit purchase receipts and vehicle-specific equipment; Slingshot items stay in their existing save fields without applying to the Ryker. The bike itself is available for every existing event without an equipment purchase.

The original `MODS.md` describes the earlier visual-only candidate and is historical. The new stock part map is bound to the preserved master's SHA-256. It exports exact position/material/normal/UV corner partitions, with source islands recorded individually. The complete vehicle is composed before paint, motion, instruments, lights or effects bind. Only geometry with the same rigid transform is merged; springs use axial deformation and shafts telescope separately.
