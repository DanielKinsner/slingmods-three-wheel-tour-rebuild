P10B — ARTWORK PRODUCTION BRIEF
This is the brief for the local implementation agent, not an already
produced or remotely generated texture. An original generated room
DEMONSTRATION is supplied alongside it.

Create one flat, front-facing panoramic wall-art plate for the existing
blank white SIDE WALL of the SlingMods studio. Measure the selected wall
in the actual game first; target roughly 3:1, adapting composition rather
than stretching a texture. Master approximately 6144x2048 where practical;
runtime derivative approximately 3072x1024 after testing close-up clarity.
Report actual source resolution; do not mislabel an upscaled source.

Art direction: a premium automotive studio mural. Mostly monochrome
charcoal, graphite, steel gray and muted silver. A visually continuous
journey from open coastal water and a waterfront road at the left, through
an elegant open-road transition, into layered wooded mountain ridges at
the right. Thoughtful photographic/engraved tonal depth. One restrained
SlingMods-red route/road line connects the panorama. Sophisticated,
cinematic, finely detailed, quiet enough to remain behind a real vehicle.

Leave a calm middle-right region for separate lettering. No built-in
typography. No logos. No giant luminous sign. No game UI. No vehicles.
No showroom, furniture, floor or ceiling. No perspective photograph of a
wall. No three-dimensional text/shadows baked into the plate. No map
asserting exact geography or directions. No neon cyberpunk palette or
stock icon collage.

Deliver the artwork itself and editable route/text sources. In the game,
add exact separate lettering reading BUILT TO BE YOURS. The optional
single subline is Coast to ridge. Build to drive. Use current authentic
logo assets elsewhere; do not trace generated logos.

Use available already-authorized generation only within no-spend limits,
or author the mural with owned/retained route assets in Blender and a
compositing tool. No new purchase or user media hunt is required. Inspect
at game wall scale and a real three-quarter camera before accepting it.

The original concept includes a different room arrangement, a wet-looking
floor and extra props. Those are NOT instructions: preserve our cabinet
wall, grid displays, lift, vented checker floor/red border and bay door.
Only ONE current blank side wall becomes the feature.
