## No flattening
Cinematic backgrounds are inspiration for presentation, not substitutes for current 3D assets. Implement text/buttons/HUD as live components. The wall image belongs to a real side-wall mesh; the conceptual room screenshot does not. Keep concept references outside public runtime assets.

## Inspect before accepting
Open the actual pixels, not just filenames. Compare a real capture at the matching viewport, plus a separate proposed hero composition. Retain the cabinet wall and checker floor even where W01 depicts a different floor or architecture.
