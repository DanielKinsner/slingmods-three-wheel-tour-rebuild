## Exactly three art targets

1. **Palm silhouette:** replace the obviously segmented rectangular foliage with a better Blender-authored crown/curved frond construction. Use a few coherent variants and believable trunk proportions, not more copies of the same broken silhouette. Alpha cards or a mesh/card hybrid are acceptable if correctly sorted/shadowed and efficient; use original local textures and verify oblique views. Avoid sawtooth cutouts and overly rigid radial spokes. Do not create a foliage-technology project.

2. **Road material:** remove the conspicuous repetitive bands, establish correct world-scale asphalt microdetail and restrained larger variation, and give painted edges/curbs believable roughness and wear. No height offset over land to disguise z-fighting. Keep surface geometry/collision correspondence exact. Roadway visibility and apex readability outrank texture noise. Do not add large random scratches just to make a material busy.

3. **Coherent waterfront light:** daylight should clearly model the car/curbs/warehouses without washing everything to gray; night should retain road cues while practical lights feel placed in an actual harbor rather than a row of isolated bright discs. Keep beams road-facing, pool transitions stable and shadows believable. No bloom blanket, wall-clock versus controlled-clock lighting mismatch or new expensive reflection system. Do not change the car's approved material definitions to compensate for poor environment light. Preserve HUD readability and camera sightlines.

Concentrate the payoff on the existing start/finish waterfront and let the repaired shared palm/road assets improve the other existing sections. Sparse infield/repeated buildings remain known later art tasks; do not expand scope into filling every empty area.
