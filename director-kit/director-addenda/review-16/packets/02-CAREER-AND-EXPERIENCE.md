# 02 — Own the Build: use the existing content as a game

## Two modes, no confusion

**Free configurator:** every implemented product and all current finishes are immediately available to preview, combine, inspect, compare and test. Existing saved recipes and site links stay functional. Never gate showroom previews behind race completion, credits, account signup or purchase. A locked career item should offer 'Preview in showroom' without changing ownership.

**Career:** retain existing underglow/shock purchases, credits, receipts, saved settings, story identities, Maya duel and earned Harbor access. Extend ownership/equip/remove/save to the already modeled Thermal exhaust, NRG wing and EvolutionR lower bags. Visually match the free showroom configuration. Installation must survive race, retry, garage and reload. Removing an owned part keeps it owned. Do not invent handling/horsepower bonuses for visual-only parts. The configured exhaust may use its existing bounded game-audio treatment.

Preview state and career state must not leak into each other. Race entries snapshot the appropriate mode's validated recipe. Foreign URLs/invalid options must not fabricate ownership or evade fitment validation. A career test drive may preview changes without spending, but committed career racing respects career equipment and event rules.

## A short next chapter, not a huge campaign

Add one compact **Harbor Express chapter** using the two existing routes and existing crew identities:

1. **Open It Up** — one valid Express time-trial lap/shakedown introducing acceleration and braking landmarks. Completion unlocks the represented Thermal exhaust for career purchase.
2. **Jett / Hold Your Nerve** — one-lap Express duel with the existing Jett identity and real shared-physics rival. A valid finish advances the chapter; winning adds a modest reward rather than locking out ordinary players. Completion opens the NRG wing in the career catalog.
3. **SlingMods Coastline Cup** — a short two-event aggregate using Original Harbor then Express. Show standings, next event, retry/abandon and a final result. Completion opens the represented lower storage bags plus a clear chapter-complete build summary. Winning can have a first-win bonus, not a mandatory cash-grind gate.

Use short, skippable, distinct crew lines. Preserve the existing chapter order and legacy access rather than forcing established players to repeat old content. Exact labels may be refined to fit the current script. Do not call cosmetic mods necessary for winning or storage a speed upgrade. The whole addition should be easy to understand from the event screen.

Set a transparent **fictional game-credit** table. A first-time normal progression through these events should fund the three newly earnable items without mandatory repeated grinding; stock cars remain capable of completing the chapter. Maintain meaningful optional performance/setup choice. Do not reset credits, fabricate prior wins or give a secret reward merely for migration. Expose purchase price, owned/equipped/unlocked states, first-completion versus repeat rewards and why a locked item unlocks. These are game prices, never real store prices.

Every result gets an idempotent receipt; retries get new attempt IDs. Cup event results/reward commits survive reloads without double payment. Handle mid-cup exit, crash/reload, retry, forfeits, repeated callbacks and partial writes. Do not mint a duplicate first-completion/cup reward. Recipe, route, handling-version, lap count and competition identity belong in the validated result/record contract.

## Old records and tune changes

A new driving profile must not silently compete against old-profile ghosts/times. Keep previous records discoverable but version scoreboards/ghost compatibility by tune/route/event. Preserve legitimate progression and credits. Do not make the historical pure-stock deep-equality fixtures compare deliberately different tunes. Preserve the historical test and add truthful tests for the new authorized behavior.

## UI and art

Use the current SlingMods design system, typography, red/graphite/white hierarchy and screen layout. The new career shop needs visual product cards using existing thumbnails and consistent 'Preview / Purchase / Equip / Remove / View on SlingMods' semantics. The free showroom is the hero, not a second conflicting product database. Race HUD/results should clearly identify event, lap, position and reward. Keep controller focus and readable720p layouts.

Retain the reference-set cabinets, vented floor/red strip, lift/door, cinematic exit, colors and swingarm accent rules. Do not remodel the room or wing in this assignment without a concrete rendering/fitment defect. Correct clipping, camera obstructions or material-state leakage encountered in the touched flows with before/after proof; no blanket art reapproval or darkening to conceal defects.

No new products, paid assets, new external services, downloads requiring accounts, new vehicle fleet entry, new destination or public hosting in this scope. The important expansion is ownership, events and rewards that use the content already delivered.
