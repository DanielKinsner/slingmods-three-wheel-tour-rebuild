## Reference comparison protocol

Choose front and front-three-quarter camera parameters that approximately match perspective and wheel ellipses in the corresponding photo. Compare feature positions relative to the wheels/body envelope. Keep a side/rear cross-check so the reconstruction does not merely match a single projection. This is a photo-guided modeling check, not an exact CAD fit, colorimetric paint sample or millimeter survey.

Keep before/after views at matching settings. For lighting-only comparisons hold asset and camera fixed; for geometry comparisons use the same neutral rig. Do not use a lower angle, extra blur or tighter crop to make a revision appear better without solving the named issue.

## Anti-overbuild boundary

The named corrections are the scope. Hidden engine-bay hardware, tiny fasteners, a full showroom prop collection, working infotainment, live mirror rendering, a helmeted driver and the next two vehicles are not required to earn this local checkpoint. The rear control view is for regressions; do not start a complete rear assembly redesign without a specific blocking defect.
