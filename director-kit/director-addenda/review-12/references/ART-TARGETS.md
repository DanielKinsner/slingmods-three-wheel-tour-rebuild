Establish visual plausibility in the full runtime before filling the rest of the route. References should guide scale, construction and plant anatomy; never redistribute third-party reference photography without permission.

## Verified primary-source implementation references

- Three.js material options: https://threejs.org/docs/pages/Material.html . Alpha test/masking is not the same as full blending; alpha-to-coverage requires actual MSAA. Inspect installed renderer and exported glTF; don't assume a documentation option was enabled in this game.
- Poly Haven license: https://polyhaven.com/license . Reuse already verified downloaded assets. CC0 applies to their assets, not all site text/preview photography. Keep source/derived hashes, provenance and any new bake-generation source.

No new engine, postprocessing stack or marketplace purchase is required by this packet. These references support choices; they do not certify the outcome.
