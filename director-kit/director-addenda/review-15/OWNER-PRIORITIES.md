# Current owner priorities — the product, not a simulation compliance exercise

Dan wants customers to recognize their real Slingshot, Spyder and Ryker, enjoy
confident energetic racing, and explore actual SlingMods accessories on vehicles
that look good close up. A recognizable, well-lit version of the YouTube set is the
showroom. Real product links connect the experience to the store. Campaign systems
are supporting content, not the center of the product.

The present pass finishes this experience for the existing Slingshot. The same
configuration, showroom and UI structures must be reusable for the later Spyder and
Ryker. Do not manufacture placeholder playable Can-Ams or pretend that recoloring
the Slingshot implements them. Do not build another chapter instead of these goals.

Owner observations: reverse was not available/discoverable in play; turning feels
unresponsive even with braking; acceleration rarely feels strong; frequent corners
keep experienced speeds around 70–80; there are unwanted holes/clipping/disconnected
parts and only one finish. Treat these as real customer-experience defects to
reproduce, not as grounds to argue that existing tests already passed. The old HUD
uses MPH, as verified in the submitted source. The reported maximum is not a proven
engine cap.

The five photos primarily describe the **room**, not a replacement model-year fleet.
Maintain the reference lock for the current vehicle. Improve the real room's
presentation and lighting rather than copying darker exposures or making a generic
futuristic garage. Do not claim dimensions hidden from these photos are measured.

Additional owner request during Review15: **make a serious overall SlingMods UI
pass**. Include all customer-facing screens and in-race presentation. Do not reduce
this request to inserting a logo and changing button colors. The UI must remain
useful, readable and actually connected to game state.

Free showroom preview is independent of career ownership. A customer should be able
to inspect and test a compatible build without earning game credits. The stock
vehicle must already be enjoyable; upgrades cannot be the repair for deliberately
poor base handling. All game-tuned performance stays distinct from OEM/retail claims.
