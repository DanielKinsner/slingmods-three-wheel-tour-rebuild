# Research scope

Primary retailer pages were opened on 15 September 2026 to establish the five
product identities, selected distinctions and listed 2024 R fitment. The register
summarizes those facts; it does not establish physical dimensions, product licensing,
all possible compatibility combinations, real aero/dyno data or current inventory.
The local agent should inspect the relevant installation media and manufacturer
sources before reproducing mounts or selecting options. Do not invent a product
identifier to fill an unresolved slot.

The preserved P08A design links the DDM manufacturer and its installation manual;
use those records when validating adjustment details. This director review did not
independently inspect every page of that manual. The lower organizer's current title
differs from the historical “Stealth Bags” shorthand; retain the actual SM-28919
identity and verify current wording instead of copying old memory as catalog truth.

Failed broad search/click attempts for a windshield were not promoted into a verified
product. No windshield or uncertain variant is required in this pass. The five-item
selection above is deliberate, bounded and already includes a functional suspension
system, two conspicuous exterior mods, lighting and a useful storage inspection.
