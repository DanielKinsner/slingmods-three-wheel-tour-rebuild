## One configuration contract across scenes

Use typed, versioned product definitions: stable ID, vehicle/years/options,
slot/mount, prerequisites/conflicts, authored asset references, shop URL, source
provenance, visual options, and any separately labeled game effect. Implement
conflicts generically even when the five selected items can coexist. Pairwise and
full-build combinations need actual clearance/visibility inspection; a source says
fitment but does not prove our model's mounting is correct.

Use a clear build recipe: vehicle ID, finish, product option selections, light
settings, suspension setup and handling-profile version. A preview recipe is not a
career purchase. Keep three scopes separate:
1. Career ownership, credits, certified event receipts and progress.
2. Freely saved showroom build recipes and an ephemeral draft being compared.
3. A test-drive/quick-race snapshot of that preview build, with no career payout.

Entering/leaving preview and comparing stock must preserve the original draft and
career byte-for-byte except for an explicitly requested save. Removing a career
part keeps ownership. Test drives instantiate the selected recipe without granting
ownership or routing through a hidden credit cheat. Existing easy-entry demo can
remain, but do not use its bypass flags as a substitute for a proper preview scope.

Add three named game presets: **Night Show**, **Harbor Sport**, and **Weekend Tour**.
Use explicit compatible selections from these five items and the four finishes.
Label them **Game build presets**, not retail bundles, SKUs, discounts or measured
performance packages. Let users inspect/remove every included component. An export
or copyable build summary is useful; it must not require an account/backend or
secret link state, and external links remain user-triggered.

## Keep facts and game behavior distinct

Do not assign fake horsepower to lights, bags or throttle response. Retain meaningful
suspension behavior. Exhaust appearance and an original game sound treatment are
valid without inventing a dyno gain. In this pass the wing is visual by default;
measured aero coefficients are unavailable, so do not quietly invent stability
percentages or downforce claims. Any later game-only aero option would need explicit
labeled modeling, coherent force/drag tradeoffs and its own evidence.

Manufacturers' advertised concepts are not measurements of this game. Avoid an
unexplained all-green stat bar that asserts every purchase improves every metric.
Useful comparisons are actual changes in appearance, selected setup and recorded
game behavior. No new real-world performance advice or installation tutorial is
being produced.

## Artwork and click-through

Author original editable meshes based on verified references, not lifted third-party
CAD or reference photographs pasted onto whole objects. All attachment transforms,
material options and hidden approximation limits are documented. Show a full-car
before/after and a close installed view, including bags within their compartments.
Actual storage openings can be animated for inspection; do not let a preview conceal
the entire car permanently. Full vehicle visibility must return on exit/driving.

Each item has an explicit **View on SlingMods** external link. **Shop This Build**
shows a digest of selected compatible products and their individual links. Open one
chosen page safely, preserving local build and navigation state. No automatic
multi-tab opening, unsupported cart assembly, store writes, sign-ups or payments.
Product thumbnails should preferably be rendered from the authored assets; source
photos remain reference-only unless rights permit the specific runtime use.
