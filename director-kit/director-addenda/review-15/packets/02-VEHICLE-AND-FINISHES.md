# 02 — The customer's Slingshot, close enough to inspect

## Repair the asset, not the screenshot

Use the current 2024 Slingshot R AutoDrive reference lock and editable source. Dan's
room photos contain different vehicles/finishes; do not blend their generations
into a fictional current model. Preserve the existing identity and good work.

Inspect the actual exported/runtime vehicle in neutral light through a full orbit,
front/rear/side/cockpit views and useful low angles. Build a named defect map:
accidental holes, unconnected shell edges, reversed normals, zero-thickness exposed
panels, intersecting shells, gaps at transitions, tire/body overlap and incorrect
moving linkages. For each suspected opening, compare the correct real-vehicle
references: genuine wheel wells, cockpit openings, vents and panel gaps must remain.

Make local mesh/topology/normal/material corrections, with editable source updates
and before/after views. Double-sided materials are not a universal hole repair.
Do not put a black patch behind everything, hide defects in exposure, or remove
parts to get a clean screenshot. Prefer coherent shared boundaries and actual
panel thickness where visible. Retain the best already-correct silhouette.

Retain world scale/contact geometry unless a narrow verified error requires change.
Steering, driver reach, suspension pose and mounts must survive the stronger driving
profile, the complete current ±20 mm modeled setup range and expected suspension
travel. Specifically retest installed DDM front carrier and rear linkage after shell
repair; do not restore the old rear tire/firewall intersection. Nothing should clip
only because the showroom is stationary during inspection.

Same canonical vehicle/configuration feeds garage, free preview, test drive and
race. LOD/detail differences may reduce cost but not move attachment locations,
change a finish, delete a selected accessory or expose a new seam. Keep suitable
close-detail only where it contributes at actual display size. Repeated geometry
and shared materials are preferable to duplicated full cars for each paint option.

## Four real, selectable game finishes

Retain the existing blue/orange finish. Add three distinctly art-directed choices:
- Gloss black with restrained SlingMods-red accents.
- Pearl/clean white with graphite details and restrained red accents.
- Satin graphite with a contrasting red accent treatment.

These are **game/custom finishes**, not asserted factory paint codes or separately
sold retail wrap products. Respect material separation: body panels/graphics can
change; rubber, seats, glass, lamp lenses, metal and logos keep their identities.
Do not tint a whole GLB blue/red. Correct masks, color spaces, roughness and material
instances matter. Stock comparison and per-part selection must not permanently
mutate shared materials used by rivals or other slots.

Allow a clear finish picker and save it into the correct user configuration. Show
the exact same selected finish in the test drive and on return. Product appearance
options are separate from paint: do not change suspension spring or exhaust tip
colors to an option that is not actually offered.

## What close inspection looks like

Default: fully assembled car, no test driver obstructing the showroom interior.
Allow a driver-visibility toggle in the garage; the driving version restores the
proper driver and controls. Framing preserves room context and makes the vehicle
large enough to judge. Optional selected-part cutaway may fade/remove only the
necessary covering region, retain mounting context and carry a clear inspection
label. The P08A floating isolated-spring view remains useful for technical comparison
but is not the default customer presentation.

Acceptance is actual multi-angle screenshots and motion, including all finishes,
stock/full-mod configurations, strong steering, compression/extension and cockpit
visibility. Automated mesh validity helps catch errors; it cannot certify the car
looks right. If a named area remains provisional, identify it specifically rather
than certifying global realism.
