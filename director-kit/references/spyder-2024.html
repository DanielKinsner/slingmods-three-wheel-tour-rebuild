Apple CarPlay

Meet Apple CarPlay

Apple CarPlay is a smarter, safer way to use your iPhone while you drive. You can get directions, make calls, send and receive messages, and enjoy your favorite music, all on your vehicle built-in display.

Learn more

MIGHTY MOTOR

ROTAX 1330 ACE engine

Spyder F3 responds to your every command: are you touring up to 252 miles (406 km) on a single tank* or using its Rotax 3-cylinder engine for maximum torque on winding roads?

*BRP internal testing

Watch video

HIGH-TECH TRACTION

Vehicle Stability System

Rider-focused safety suite: stability control developed in partnership with BOSCH†, anti-lock brakes (ABS), plus traction control are the confidence-inspiring features that keep Spyder F3 planted to the road.

Watch video

CONFIDENT STEERING

Dynamic electric assist for all riders

Turn with ease: Electronically-controlled power steering makes handling the Spyder F3 a joy, and responds with more or less assistance depending on your speed.

DIGITAL THEFT PROTECTION

Standard anti-theft system for Spyder

Kept on lock: Digitally Encoded Security System (D.E.S.S.) protects the Can-Am Spyder F3 from theft & unauthorized use. Without the specific coded D.E.S.S. key, a Spyder F3 won’t start.

FINGERTIP SHIFTS

Standard semi-automatic transmission

Shift your way with index finger & thumb control paddle shifter—or just cruise. A hydraulic clutch offers the perfect balance of comfort and fuel economy.

Watch video

SUPER STOPS

World-class Brembo brake system

No-fade performance: Brembo’s Spyder-specific braking system is a non-negotiable upgrade that delivers impressive stopping power and consistency in all conditions.

STRETCH EVERY MILE

Standard ECO Mode Smart Assist

Whether it’s for a relaxed daily commute or to stretch fuel stops on a road trip, ECO Mode Smart Assist helps you optimize your shifting behaviour to spend more time riding—and less time at the pump.
