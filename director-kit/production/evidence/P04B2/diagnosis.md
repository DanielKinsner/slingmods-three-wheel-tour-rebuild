# P04B2 diagnosis and bounded repair

The Review08 capture stall was reproduced without recording, preserved drawing buffers or the full inspection callback. On the RTX 4080 / ANGLE Direct3D 11 host, baseline-clean showed a 3,982.8 ms render submission when resident visible spotlights changed from five to six (two headlights plus three/four practicals). Program count increased from 14 to 27. The next RAF interval was 3,983.2 ms. This is a production-path problem, not solely recorder overhead.

The initial GL wrapper did not cover the blocking method, so its absence of slow calls was inconclusive. The expanded baseline-diagnostic-gl wrapper covered shader information queries. Thirteen getProgramInfoLog calls, individually 235–421 ms, occurred at the same new-program transition inside a roughly 3.94-second render. Its next RAF interval was 3,949.9 ms. See the complete event list and raw rows in that run.json. These queries synchronize first use of newly linked shader variants; disabling diagnostics or removing recording alone does not repair the changing program configuration.

Installed Three.js 0.186.0 sources were inspected locally. WebGLRenderer gathers visible lights, WebGLPrograms puts spot/rect-area counts in cache parameters, and RectAreaLight power is intensity × width × height × pi. The version was retained. No renderer or dependency migration was made.

The fix retains all four practical slots, keeping the original continuous fade and position updates with zero intensity outside range. Two existing product area-light slots also remain resident at zero intensity when unequipped/off. Hardware remains detached when unequipped. Resident light slots and emitting lights are explicitly separate diagnostics. No additional shadow maps or world-light/exposure/material edits were introduced.

Before the driving menu becomes ready, a visible preparation veil covers texture initialization, compileAsync, an actual all-mesh render (including shadow/depth first use), and one loading-only GPU synchronization. Original frustum-culling settings are restored in finally. The simulation/race is not advanced by preparation. Total loading and individual preparation stages are retained; this work was moved into an honest loading state, not excluded from accounting. No driver/OS shader-cache clearing is claimed. A fresh browser/context is application-cold, not guaranteed driver-cache-cold.

One shader/lighting approach was evaluated, followed by a bounded final refinement for an asynchronous equipment update during preparation and the bay loading-message timing. There was no second renderer strategy or unbounded rewrite. The helper independently found these issues, confirmed the repairs and reviewed the first full-course timing result. The full final trial matrix and video are separate evidence.

The diagnostic baseline used the reviewed implementation plus measurement seams (lightweight getter, opt-in bounded logger, buffer flag split). Its run.json parent commit identifies that baseline, not a pristine uninstrumented working tree. Baseline runtime files were not frozen as a separate committed build. This limits exact byte reproduction of those short historical diagnostics; their raw timings, GL calls and unchanged baseline light implementation remain available. Final measurements have explicit source-input and served-asset manifests.

The runtime fix does not establish universal GPU headroom or human hardware/aural approval. RAF intervals include scheduling; CPU submission measurements are not GPU timer-query results. Every recorded outlier, preparation duration and bounded-buffer overflow count is retained.
