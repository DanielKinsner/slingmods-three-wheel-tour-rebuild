## Previously rejected defects and repairs

The straight-ramp uncommanded turn was an actual defect, not explained away by green broad tests. Read the retained failed stricter test and instrumented guard/ground-normal records: a shallow collision against the large convex slab produced an almost horizontal normal and4005.86Ns impulse. Controlled sensor/friction/ground variants localized that response. The repair replaces the ground top with finite planar triangles and makes non-spinning guard proxies frictionless, retaining their collision-normal response and the custom tire traction law. No yaw clamp, pose reset, rail or global stabilizer was introduced. The same new every-tick3degree/0.5m bound was kept, and three symmetric spawn runs now stay within0.01844m and0.02958degrees. Independent rerun of that regression passed. The final incline movie agrees with straight recovery.

The first cockpit steering captures were blank because the absolute inspection camera stayed at the origin while the car was near z35. This reviewer rejected them. They remain in presenter-revision01; corrected harness resets near the inspection origin. Final PNGs were opened directly and contain the vehicle, cabin and steering wheel. This is a capture-fixture repair, not a claimed gameplay camera repair.

## Material limits retained

The high-speed test drives squarely into a vertical18cm curb at21.66m/s (roughly48mph). Its approximately3.48m flight and pronounced attitude change are accepted here only as a bounded severe-impact approximation: the car contacts the obstacle, departs support, lands, regains all three supports and brakes, rather than tunneling, continuing an unexplained spin or becoming stuck. Ordinary curb and incline behavior are substantially milder. This does not validate real crash dynamics, damage, rider safety or OEM suspension response. A3.48m leap must not be presented as realistic product proof, and subsequent course design/tuning should avoid making severe vertical impacts a normal driving mechanic.

These browser videos are slow SwiftShader correctness evidence: roughly23–29wall seconds for9–12simulation seconds, with repeated captured frames and elapsed-time clamping. No actual30/60/120/144Hz presentation or physical GPU performance pass is granted. Normal-speed physical-GPU driving, controller/mobile tests, full camera set, dynamic suspension-link geometry, rider contact, audio, final materials/lights, performance and enjoyment remain later gates. Visible shadow-map bands and unfinished clay geometry are presentation defects for G3, not concealed as finished quality.
