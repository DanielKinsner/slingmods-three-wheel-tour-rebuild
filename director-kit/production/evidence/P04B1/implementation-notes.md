# P04B1 implementation and reference limits

Current frozen implementation: dd2c3f9d3b917aec1a7f2e6750f93afe17505292. Prior accepted Review07 packaging: b51c846c173f4bdfd9772d265e80e0b472f66dac. The local tree continued in the same rebuild. No reset, scenery/body edit, dependency update, spending or deployment.

The career reducer runs inside one IndexedDB readwrite transaction per command. Unique v4 attempt IDs are minted on the existing actual reset/start hook; the unchanged race tick validates the finish, then dispatches a once-only award. Both 800/100 amounts and 600 price are fixed in local logic. Owned equipment/color mutations load the latest committed record; BroadcastChannel and focus refresh update other tabs. Old settings/PB decoder and namespace are untouched, with no retroactive award. Offline local state is user-editable, not anti-cheat.

On blocked IndexedDB, the same reducer uses tab memory. A one-shot window.name envelope carries it only across deliberate same-origin in-game scene navigation, is consumed immediately and restores the prior window name. Nothing is put in the URL or sent to the real product link. This is not durable storage: independent reload/new tab may lose it, and the UI says Session only. A post-boot transaction failure leaves old durable balance/ownership intact and reports failure; the user may retry. External HTTPS linking uses native target=_blank, rel=noopener noreferrer and no cart/API integration.

The accessory is separate Blender geometry: four visible mounting regions, 2,368triangles/2draws/zero textures/89,992bytes. Exact 2024 SM-133 fitment was verified on the primary page by the artist. Its linked earlier-generation installation video establishes regions, not exact2024 routing. The rendered side runs are1.2m and hidden continuation/receiver/harness are omitted. This is a visual approximation, not an installation model. See artist/HANDOFF.md and actual mesh/road-clearance reports.

Two evaluated lighting constructions: initial downward spot prototype made small point-like hot spots; the second uses two narrow downward RectAreaLights aligned with the side strips, plus the original emissive diffuser material. No shadow maps, decals, bloom, car material or environment-light changes outside temporary bay preview. Area-light integration adds lookup/shader resources; measure actual renderer residency separately. Four strip regions use only two side area emitters, so front strip pavement output is simplified. Surface geometry/normals and light falloff are real runtime shading, but underside/curb occluder shadows are absent. The global road/headlights remain unchanged.

Current bay A/B makes red/cyan strip contribution legible and restores normal light values on exit. From the bright Harbor near chase, the underglow is subdued and may be visually lost under practical/headlight wash. The result is a functioning cosmetic feature with a specific lighting-readability HOLD, not a claim of finished night product presentation. Two evaluated lighting constructions exhausted; no further body/scenery/lighting iteration in this packet.

Build uses native focus/click, the existing DeviceSample for controller navigation, and neutral-release gating. A compact scrollable left card leaves the whole car framed on the right. On narrow screens details scroll; no claim of broad mobile/device QA. New Rae text is brief and skippable at first entry/result/build only. There is no voice, opponent, full chapter or performance product.
