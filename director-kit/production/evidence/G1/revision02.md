# First lead review repair

The lead rejected three primary-form weaknesses in revision01: surface-like central lamp, missing raised hood inlet, and a tube-shaped splitter. Revision01 source is preserved under `assets/blender/vehicles/revision01/`; its exported GLB, build script and dimensional manifest are under this evidence folder's `revision01/`.

Revision02 removes the central leading hood faces to create a real trapezoidal opening. The cavity has recessed back, roof, lower tray and sidewalls; the lamp is set back inside, with a separate black housing. The hood inlet now has a raised shaped roof, sidewalls, dark recessed throat and lower lip. The splitter is now a broad 95 mm fore-aft blade with 26 mm thickness and a restrained chamfer following the original stepped front outline.

The result was exported from Blender and numerically rechecked against unchanged shared contacts. Triangles: 34,688. Overall bounds: 1.996 m wide, 3.799 m long, 1.313 m tall. `blender-diagnostic-*.png` was regenerated for this revision, labeled as Blender-only evidence. New runtime capture and separate reviewer acceptance remain necessary.
