## Runtime art scope and verdict

Viewed the actual eight full-size day/night district frames plus overview and dock in rollout-review-05. Its asset-provenance.json reports stable before/after hashes. These images precede the artist's final ce73e02 planting-bed/hull correction; that subsequent art change must be represented by the final captures, not treated as already visually approved here.

The roadway now reads as fine asphalt and the barriers as pale cast concrete. The sky has actual outdoor variation, and the car/road-white highlights stay controlled. Night practical pools are more restrained than early benchmarks while markings remain readable. The water has smaller ripples and a shore/deep response, and the connected quay/pier/terminal frontage provides more depth than the original color-block kit. Shore land and apron surfaces are now present without the major disappearance/z-fighting failures of benchmark04. The filled distant mainland removes the large open sky wedge seen in the earlier overview.

The visual target is still not fully demonstrated by these intermediate frames. Service and promenade remain dominated by broad flat grass with widely separated buildings; the ordinary camera does not yet show a rich, naturally connected district throughout the route. The overview is technically coherent but still visibly simple. Palms have real leaflet negative space but retain regular, wiry silhouettes at medium distance. Background facades and the photographed dock boats remain basic, with boat hulls explicitly under final repair. Tan planting patches in these images are also under repair. Reflection is the sky probe, so buildings/boats do not appear as scene reflections in water; no such reflection is claimed. Night benefits from lighting but does not erase these daylight limitations.
