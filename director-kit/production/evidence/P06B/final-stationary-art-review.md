## Observed strengths and remaining gaps

The technical water/ground occlusion and missing-island failures from the earlier benchmark are absent in this final overview. Water connects visibly to the quay, docks sit at the shoreline, and the former sky wedge is filled. Depth precision and terrain integration repair are credible; that does not make the overall art target pass.

Terminal is the strongest district: photo-textured pavement and pale cast barriers, curb/drain transitions, building entrances, shelter, dock connection and planted frontage support the place. Marina now contains shaped boat hulls instead of rectangular hull blocks. Foreground dock planks, railings, waterfront stone and water detail read as different surfaces. Water still has regular repeating ripples, flat distant specular color, and no reflected local boats/buildings; those are visible approximation limits.

The daylight sky has coherent cloud structure and directional shadows. Neutral concrete is reasonably readable, and the blue car remains identifiable. Night is visually distinct with useful warm road pools and a cool sky. Unlit landscaping and car backs become very dark in some views. This is not a final color or aural approval.

Across the service and promenade views the target remains **not fully met**: broad flat lawns, widely spaced simple facades and thin repeated palms leave the world sparse and artificial. The asphalt's repeated tile pattern remains visible along the road. Far buildings form a repeated strip with little depth or connective context. Close palm detail shows a trunk and individual fronds, but middle-distance foliage reads wiry. Darker planting beds improve the earlier tan cutout appearance without solving the sparse landscape composition. The overview makes the thin development and large rectangular apron/land masses especially obvious.

The compact garage is materially quieter and the car has a visible contact/cast shadow against the floor. Stock versus installed/night comparisons are legible, the UI has a usable hierarchy and the cyan product is clear. The floor remains very bright with large slab seams, and the room is still a simple inspection space rather than a rich showroom. Front vehicle fidelity is outside this packet's allowed revisions and is not newly approved here.

These are honest runtime improvements and preserved functioning comparison paths. They do not establish an environment quality lock or G3/G4 approval. The final artwork remains held for Astra's judgment. No additional source or asset changes were made during this review.
