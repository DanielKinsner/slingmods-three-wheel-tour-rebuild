# Phase 2B — time of day (2026-09-21)

1. `src/presentation/time-of-day.ts`: five LOOKS for the flat routes: Day, Golden hour, Dusk, Night, After rain. A look is presentation only (2K HDR sky, sun/hemisphere/fog re-colour of the validated rig, exposure, bloom, a colour grade). Each sits on one of the two validated bases (`day`|`night`); `preset=look.base` so records, saves, lamps, lit windows and vehicle optics key on exactly what they always did. Day and Night ARE the untouched old looks (no sky, rig or grade override). Career events and the ridge never change look.
2. Default is Dusk on Express and Harbor (owner decision 3A). Choice is per route, remembered under its own key `slingmods-look-v1` (not the game save), forced by `?look=`. Selector: "Time" next to "Graphics" in the race menu; it restarts the scene only from the start menu, otherwise applies next drive. Harbor's default moves to After rain once the wet road (2C) exists; After rain is dry for now and only flags `wet:true`.
3. Skies: `scripts/p11/build-skies-2k.py` area-averages the 8K masters (60-77 MB) to 2048x1024 (4-5 MB) for golden-hour, dusk, after-rain; Night keeps the analytic sky. HDR looks go through the same sun-capped probe path as the validated day sky. Honest note: the P11 "dusk" photo is a pale overcast, so the mood comes from the rig, lamps, bloom and grade, not the photograph.
4. Colour grade added to the composite pass (scene-linear tint, saturation, contrast about 18% grey, before tone mapping). Trackside sodium lamps and lit windows follow `look.lampsOn`.
5. Failure paths: a missing sky falls back to the base look; skies are optional downloads and on the hosted allowlist (the allowlist test covers them).
