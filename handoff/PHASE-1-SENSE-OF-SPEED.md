# Phase 1 — sense of speed, express then harbor (2026-09-21)

1. Camera (`src/presentation/speed-feel.ts`, layered after the validated chase camera): FOV 55 -> 78 deg, trails/drops under throttle, tucks under braking, looks toward the velocity vector, rolls <= 2 deg with lateral g; cockpit head lean + buzz. Edge blur from 70 mph and 0.5-1.5 px shake from 90 mph (more off asphalt) via the first post pass (`speed-post.ts`). Reduced motion = bit-identical old camera and no post target; `?quality=low` skips the post pass. Presentation only: no physics, input, timing or record changes.
2. Road: P11 race asphalt at 4 m / 4096 px + 0.5 m detail normal; decal layer (rubber line, seams, tar snakes, patches, cracks, worn paint, lock-ups, launch marks) in two draw calls. Source fixes first: the asphalt scan was 1.5% albedo (`scripts/p11/calibrate-asphalt-albedo.py` -> `track-4m-baseColor`), and the armco was near-black IN ENGINE because the steel trim band was a rusty scan flagged fully metallic (`build-atlases.py`, then `sync-shared-trim.py` re-points the 66 prop GLBs).
3. Trackside (`trackside.ts`, `place-route.ts` = typed port of the P11 helper): express gets armco + red-white jersey exactly on the existing 11.2 m collision rail line, lights every 40 m alternating, catch fence, 150/100/50 boards at detected braking zones (stations 800 and 1720). Harbor keeps its own barriers/lamps/paint and gains asphalt, decals, fence and boards. Instanced, per-instance LOD 0/1/2 by distance, zero colliders added. Old express canvas brake boards and flat white paint are hidden, not deleted.
4. Frame time, uncapped 2560x1440, RTX 4080, standard quality, near view, full throttle 2.5-8.5 s. Before: express 12.4-16.9 ms avg (1% low 43-109), harbor 19.4-23.9 (45-59). After (3 runs): express 4.9 / 6.0 / 7.3 ms (1% low 27-34), harbor 7.4 / 9.3 / 32.5 (36-148). The gain is mostly the Phase 0 mirror gate: the wider FOV takes the mirrors under 40 px at speed. EVERY remaining spike >25 ms happens while the mirrors are live (0-30 mph); far view on harbor = 5.0 ms avg, zero spikes in 9 s. The mirrors are the performance HOLD, not this work.
5. Checks: typecheck clean, 315/315 tests (9 new in `tests/phase1-speed.test.ts` + mirror view-target), production build OK, P11 pack validation 6/6, headless 1440p drives on both routes with zero page errors. Adds ~41 MB of KTX2 downloads per flat route (pre-warmed by the showroom). Not touched: water, trees, skies, UI, gameplay, ridge. Known: holding throttle with no steering drifts the car right onto the run-off by ~400 m on express (pre-existing handling).
