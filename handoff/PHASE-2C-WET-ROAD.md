# Phase 2C — wet road, puddles, underglow reflections (2026-09-21)

1. Wetness lives in the road material (`race-asphalt.ts`, only when the look is wet; dry roads keep their unchanged shader): the whole surface darkens and turns glossy, and where the P11 puddle mask is high the water stands, goes mirror-smooth and flat. Built on the CALIBRATED dry maps; the P11 `wet-*` maps are not used because they derive from the 1.5%-albedo scan. Decals, paint, kerbs and run-off turn glossy with it. VISUAL ONLY: a test asserts the simulation and course code know nothing about wet or puddles; grip, physics and records are unchanged.
2. `wet-reflection.ts` (High/Ultra): one half-resolution top-level pass from the camera mirrored about the road. It draws ONLY render-layer 1: player car, rivals, underglow, trackside props, plus every light. Road, ground, water and buildings are not on that layer, so the underground mirrored camera is never blocked and no clipping is needed. The sky is left out (the material already mirrors the sky probe) and the image's alpha lets reflected objects cover that glint. Live mirror faces are excluded. Medium/Low: puddles mirror the sky only.
3. The underglow already had two real downward strip lights, so on a glossy road they reflect as strips with no tricks; bloom does the rest. Daytime wet bloom is deliberately restrained (threshold 1.6); dusk/night wet is not.
4. Looks: added `dusk-rain` and `night-rain` (identical to their dry look except `wet` and bloom; pinned by a test). Defaults are now Express = Dusk after rain, Harbor = After rain. Puddle mask is an optional download and on the hosted allowlist.
5. Cost, measured with draw-call counting (uncapped 1440p, RTX 4080, first seconds of a drive with mirrors live): harbor already issues ~1,490 draw calls per frame; the reflection pass adds ~250, mostly the player car's many small meshes. Every frame that cost ~5 ms on harbor, so High refreshes it every OTHER frame (the image is sampled through the matrix it was drawn with, so the world stays put; only the car's own reflection trails by one frame) and Ultra every frame. After that: harbor dusk dry 10.6 vs wet 11.1 (High), 12.7 (Ultra); express 8.0 vs 9.9. Honest status against the budget (High <= 10 ms avg): express meets it; harbor at dusk sits at ~10-11 ms on this (busy) machine, and that is the dusk/night light rig plus harbor's draw-call count, not the wet road. Harbor draw calls are the next performance target. Benchmarks today were noisy (other load on the PC, and my own preview tab rendering the showroom on the same GPU until I parked it).
Checks: typecheck clean, 328/328 tests (1 new + updates in tests/phase2-time-of-day.test.ts and phase2-pipeline.test.ts), production build OK, hosted package rebuilt: both flat routes reach the drive with 0 failed requests.
