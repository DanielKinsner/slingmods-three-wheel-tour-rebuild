Newly mapped surfaces use metre-based projection, including instance scale; concrete tiles at 2 m, metal at 0.5 m, wood/rock at 1.8 m and the retained lawn scan at 2 m. Painted colors, metalness and wet-look roughness are preserved. Explicit scenery names protect vehicle finishes, logos/sign graphics, the Tour Wall, glass/emissives, water and forest shaders. Atmospheric distant Ridge silhouettes remain part of the existing sky treatment.

Materials are shared between matching batches, so this adds no meshes, geometry, draw calls or render passes. Disposal restores original bindings before releasing owned materials/maps.
