# Phase 2F - driving contact and stable chase shadows

- Story, quick races and test runs share one contact-shadow mesh: three tire patches and a chassis patch per car. Read-only rays query the real static ground, including Ridge slopes; patches fade with ground clearance and disappear on overturned cars. The player patch follows the interpolated visual pose. Result views reuse these patches, avoiding double contact shading. No physics, steering/input, save, product or reward changes.
- The existing single sun map now follows the chase view on a snapped light-space grid. Low/Medium/High/Ultra use 512/1024/2048/2048 resolution, soft PCF filtering and a bounded 52/48/48/48 m footprint. Quality switches update the map live and dispose old targets. Contact shading uses one extra draw per scene pass, no new render pass, texture downloads or dependencies. `&contact=off` is the contact-only comparison switch. This is local contact occlusion plus one focused sun map, not screen-space AO or multiple shadow cascades; that choice keeps the existing race budget bounded.
